# Coin-cell organizer — v1.0

7 each, 35 total / approx. 96.4 × 117 × 28.9mm

CR1632 / CR2016 / CR2025 / CR2032 / CR2450

Print settings: PLA Basic · 0.4mm nozzle · 0.2mm for all layers · 3 walls · 20% infill · no supports.

Prepare four Ø5×2mm magnets and a Ø1.75mm filament pin. Pockets are Ø5.3×2.2mm. No glue or separate plugs are needed.

Keep the supplied print orientations; do not mirror the lid. After cooling, insert the filament pin and trim its ends without obstructing rotation.

- Lid: Before layer 58 · 11.4mm completed — Insert 2 magnets

- Body: Before layer 84 · 16.6mm completed — Insert 2 magnets with polarity attracting the lid

Seat every magnet entirely below the next layer. Watch for magnets lifting when printing resumes.

The 3MF includes pauses; STLs do not. Recheck if orientation, scale or layer height changes. The lid-only 3MF pauses once.

Photos show the actual print. The fixed parts were printed and used by the designer. The complete 3MF combines the successful body and corrected lid; its slicing was checked separately. This is not a quantified shake, drop or lifetime test.

This is not a child-resistant container. Keep coin cells and magnets out of children's reach.
