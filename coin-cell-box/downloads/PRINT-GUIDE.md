## Files
- coincell-tested-full-PLA.3mf: complete body + corrected lid. P1S 0.4mm / PLA Basic / 0.2mm. Two pauses included.
- coincell-tested-lid-PLA.3mf: replacement lid only. One pause included.
- coincell-tested-body.stl and coincell-tested-lid.stl: same saved meshes used for the successful physical parts. STL does not include pauses.

The complete 3MF combines the separately printed successful parts; the combined plate is slicer-verified, not a separately tested physical print. Choose your own actual printer and PLA slot before sending.

## Capacity and parts
CR1632, CR2016, CR2025, CR2032, CR2450: 7 each, 35 total.
Overall design envelope approximately 96.38 × 117 × 28.85mm.
20° tilted slots, 9mm centres. Lid pads follow the different cell heights with nominal 0.5mm clearance.
Four Ø5 × 2mm magnets; cavities Ø5.3 × 2.2mm.
Ø1.75mm filament for the hinge pin; trim to fit without obstructing rotation.

## Printing and magnet insertion
Use the supplied orientations: body bottom on bed, lid exterior on bed. Do not mirror the lid.
PLA Basic, 0.2mm first and subsequent layers, no supports, 3 walls, 20% infill.
At 0.2mm constant layer height:

| Part | Completed height | Pause before layer | Magnets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lid | 11.4mm | 58 (Z11.6mm) | 2 |
| Body | 16.6mm | 84 (Z16.8mm) | 2 |

At the first pause, place two magnets into the open lid pockets, entirely below the next layer. Resume to seal them.
At the second pause, insert the two body magnets with polarity that attracts the corresponding lid magnets. Check polarity before insertion. Resume to seal.
Lid-only project stops only once. Variable layer height, a different first layer or a changed scale invalidates these layer numbers. Check the sliced cavity roof before printing.
After cooling, assemble with the filament pin. Lift a cell along its tilted slot before pinching it free.

## What was verified
The designer reports the corrected physical box is usable and supplied photos of the open/closed box and cell removal. Stored-mesh assembly, sampled opening and 0.5mm nominal lid gaps were checked. No quantified shake/drop/lifetime result is claimed. Custom editor outputs are different designs and remain physically untested.
This is an organizer, not a child-resistant battery container. Keep coin cells and loose magnets away from children.
